Our Background

10+ years turning messy data into business decisions

DISHA was founded after years spent inside fast-growing companies watching the same problem repeat: leadership teams making six-figure decisions on spreadsheets nobody fully trusted. Pipelines broke silently. Dashboards disagreed with each other. Every "quick" data request took two weeks.

We started DISHA to fix that properly — not with another tool to buy, but with the strategy, engineering, and discipline to make data a dependable foundation instead of a recurring fire drill. Every engagement is led by senior consultants who've sat on both sides of the table: as the people building the pipelines, and as the executives waiting on the numbers.

Why We Started DISHA

Because "we'll pull a report" shouldn't take a week

Three patterns kept showing up everywhere we worked — so we built a firm specifically to solve them.

01

Slow answers, fast decisions

Businesses move fast; their data teams often can't keep up. We close that gap with self-serve systems, not more middlemen.
