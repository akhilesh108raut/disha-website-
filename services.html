FAQ

Common questions

Which tier should I start with? +

If you're unsure what's broken in your data, start with Foundation — it's the fastest way to get a clear, prioritized picture before committing to a build.

Do you work with our existing tools, or do we need to switch? +

We're tool-agnostic. We'll recommend changes only where your current stack is genuinely limiting you — never to sell a specific platform.

What happens after the engagement ends? +

Every project includes documentation and training so your team can operate independently. Enterprise clients keep an ongoing retainer for continued support.
