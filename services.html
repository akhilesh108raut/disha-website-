FAQ

Common questions

Which engagement should I start with? +

If you don't yet have a validated use case, start with a Diagnostic Engagement — it's the fastest way to get a funded, prioritized roadmap before committing to a build.

Do you only work with a specific industry? +

No. Our team has delivered across manufacturing, healthcare, retail, finance, energy, education, and SaaS. The methodology is industry-agnostic; the use case is specific to you.

How do you handle security and data access? +

Your data stays in your environment wherever possible. Access is scoped to the engagement and formally revoked at handoff — nothing lingers after we're done.

What if the use case doesn't pan out? +

That's what the Diagnostic and PoC stages are for — validating feasibility and ROI before you commit to a full build. Killing a bad use case early is a successful outcome, not a failure.

Do you work with our existing tools, or do we need to switch? +

We're tool-agnostic across cloud providers, data platforms, and model vendors. We'll recommend changes only where your current stack is genuinely limiting you — never to sell a specific platform.

What happens after the engagement ends? +

Every project includes full documentation and team training so you can operate independently. Enterprise Partnership clients keep an ongoing retainer for continued support — it's optional, not required.

Can scope and pricing flex for our situation? +

Yes — the figures above are typical starting points. We confirm an exact, fixed price and scope after the discovery call, before any work begins.
